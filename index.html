Morning Briefing

A curated daily digest, generated locally.

One Python script, one HTML page, one word in the terminal: briefing. It fetches the biotech and health-tech headlines I actually read, tracks the stocks and private companies I'm watching, and keeps a persistent notebook for the daily Paul Graham essay — all in a single dashboard that opens in under eight seconds.

What's inside

Live news grid

Pharma, biotech, and health-tech headlines from the last 36 hours, plus the latest podcast episodes — arranged in a 3×2 grid you can read without scrolling.

Stocks

Public tickers I follow, grouped into Market & Tech and Pharma & Health, with one-day percent change color-coded.

Private-company watchlist

Google News headlines for private companies I care about, with query enrichment and topic filtering so I don't get noise.

Reading Journal

A rotating Paul Graham essay each day, plus a persistent notebook keyed by essay URL. Notes auto-save to the browser and survive daily regenerations.

Why these sources (and not others)

A deliberate choice: only vetted, named publications.

Every news source on this dashboard is a real publication with editors and bylines. There is no social media firehose, no AI-generated content, no SEO affiliate farms, and no generic aggregators. If a source can't be trusted on its worst day, it doesn't appear on this page.

  • Endpoints News
    pharma, R&D, health-tech trade reporting
  • Fierce Biotech
    biotech industry publication
  • Huberman Lab
    long-form health science podcast
  • Acquired
    long-form company-history podcast

For watchlist companies, Google News is the source, but with two defenses against noise: a per-company query that disambiguates short names (searching "Ro" telehealth pharmacy weight loss instead of Ro), and a topic-keyword post-filter that drops headlines without sector-relevant terms. Articles that look like a company but aren't about one are removed before I ever see them.

Explicitly excluded: Twitter/X, Reddit, LinkedIn, anonymous blog aggregators, AI-generated news digests, and any outlet whose business model is clickbait or affiliate commission.

How it works

1 · Fetch

Parallel pull

RSS feeds, Yahoo Finance tickers, and Google News run concurrently so total wait time is the slowest single response, not the sum.

2 · Filter

Freshness & relevance

News older than 36 hours is dropped. Watchlist headlines pass through a topic-keyword filter before rendering.

3 · Render

Single HTML file

The script writes one self-contained page with inline CSS and JS, no frameworks, no accounts, no cloud. Open it in any browser.
